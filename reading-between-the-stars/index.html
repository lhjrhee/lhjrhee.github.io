Reading Between the Stars

Extracting Pre-Purchase Expectations from Fashion Product Reviews Using Large Language Models

Hyunjeong “Lacy” Rhee1, Xinyi Bao2, and Li Zhao1

1 University of Missouri   2 University of Pittsburgh

What did shoppers expect?

A review can describe both what a shopper anticipated and what they experienced. Guided by expectation-confirmation theory (Oliver, 1980), this study uses a large language model to extract expectation-related content from fashion reviews and examines how its topics vary across star ratings.

4,845cleaned JD.com dress reviews
4,217records returned in the explicit-expectation field
87.0%model extraction rate, not validated accuracy

Chinese-language reviews, posted in 2024–2026, were sampled through JD.com’s Recommendation tab. The submitted abstract reports that 94.0% of the cleaned reviews received five stars. Results therefore require particular caution when comparing lower-rating groups.

Analysis algorithm

The workflow adapts LLM-based extraction of purchase reasons and subsequent experiences (Chen et al., 2024), then applies BERTopic to organize the extracted text (Grootendorst, 2022).

  1. Prepare review records

    Pair each review with its product information and star rating. Remove blank or meaningless review entries to obtain the cleaned sample of 4,845 reviews.

  2. Extract structured content with Gemini

    Provide Chinese product information and review text to Google Gemini. The prompt specifies the analyst’s role, research context, task, style, output format, and intended audience.

  3. Return English JSON fields

    Request explicit_expectation, implicit_expectation, expectation_explanation, and post_purchase_experience. The explanation field provides supporting textual evidence for the extraction.

  4. Select the explicit-expectation field

    Analyze the 4,217 records for which the model returned an explicit-expectation entry. Preserve the source review and rating so each extracted record remains traceable.

  5. Identify topics with BERTopic

    Group extracted expectation text through embedding-based topic modeling and inspect topic keywords. Retain outlier assignments when describing the topic distribution.

  6. Compare topics across ratings

    Tabulate topic counts and within-rating topic shares. Interpret these descriptive patterns through ECT and use them to identify product attributes for further investigation.

Interpretation boundary. The final extraction prompt allows praise of basic functional attributes to count as an explicit expectation. Its output may therefore include inferred expectations as well as directly expressed prior expectations. Human validation is needed before treating the extracted content as verified evidence of pre-purchase expectations.

View algorithm pseudocode

A conceptual description of the reported analysis, rather than executable source code.

INPUT: review records containing product information, review text, and rating

cleaned_records = remove_blank_or_meaningless_entries(records)
extracted_records = []

FOR each record IN cleaned_records:
    output = GEMINI(
        product_information = record.product_information,
        review_text = record.review_text,
        structured_extraction_prompt
    )
    # Output fields, in English:
    # explicit_expectation, implicit_expectation,
    # expectation_explanation, post_purchase_experience

    IF output.explicit_expectation contains extracted text:
        retain(record, output) IN extracted_records

texts = explicit_expectation text FROM extracted_records
assignments, topic_keywords = BERTOPIC(texts)

FOR each star rating:
    summarize topic counts and within-rating shares
    retain outlier assignments in the distribution

OUTPUT: extracted records, topic descriptions, and topic-by-rating summaries

Reproducibility: the supplied materials do not specify the Gemini model version, generation settings, or complete BERTopic configuration. This page documents the reported workflow without supplying unverified settings.

From dissatisfaction to root-cause investigation

Expectation-related review analysis can help brands identify where to investigate the gap between anticipated and received products. These signals guide diagnosis but do not establish the underlying cause on their own.

Illustrative applications, subject to confirmation through review and product checks.
Review signalWhere a brand can investigate
Color or appearance differs from online imagesCheck lighting, image editing, color descriptions, and whether photographs represent the shipped product.
Fabric feels different from what the shopper anticipatedCompare material specifications with the delivered garment and assess whether images or descriptions adequately convey texture, thickness, or drape.
Fit or sizing falls short of expectationsCheck size-chart accuracy, garment measurements, and the clarity of fit guidance.
Construction or finishing disappointsInspect production quality, defects, and consistency across product batches.

Targeted changes to product information or product quality may reduce avoidable disappointment and returns. Those outcomes were not tested in this study.
